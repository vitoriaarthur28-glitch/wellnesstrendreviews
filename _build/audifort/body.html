What Is Audifort And How Does It Work?

Audifort is a dietary supplement in liquid drops that the label calls "Supports Healthy Hearing." It is designed for adults who want to look after their hearing, mental sharpness, and daily energy as they get older, with a simple routine of one or two droppers a day.

The Supplement Facts panel lists Chromium and a 200 mg proprietary blend of more than twenty botanicals and amino acids. The official website highlights six of them: Maca Root, Grape Seed, Green Tea, Capsicum Annuum, Gymnema Sylvestre, and GABA.

The idea behind the formula is that the ear depends on good circulation and protection from oxidative stress, and that hearing and the brain work together. According to the official website, grape seed provides antioxidants, green tea supports blood flow to the ears, capsicum supports a healthy inflammatory response, maca supports energy, and GABA promotes relaxation.

Rather than acting as a quick fix, Audifort is meant to be taken every day, for months. The official website says the best results come with consistent use for four months or longer.

With consistent daily use, the customers quoted on the official website describe better sleep, more peace of mind, and sharper thinking, especially when the drops are combined with protection from loud noise, good sleep, and regular hearing check-ups.

In practical terms, Audifort is a 1 fl oz (30 ml) dropper bottle with 30 servings of 1 ml, a one-month supply.

According to the label, Audifort is made in the USA with globally sourced ingredients and manufactured in an FDA-registered facility. It is a dietary supplement, not a replacement for a hearing evaluation or a hearing aid.

Audifort Ingredients

Every 1 ml serving of Audifort contains Chromium and a 200 mg proprietary blend. These are the six ingredients the official website highlights, with the role it gives each one:

  • Maca (Lepidium meyenii) Root Extract: A Peruvian root used to support energy and stamina.

  • Grape (Vitis vinifera) Seed Extract: A source of polyphenols that provides antioxidant protection.

  • Green Tea (Camellia sinensis) Leaf Extract: Rich in catechins, associated with healthy blood flow.

  • Capsicum Annuum Fruit Extract: A pepper extract that supports a healthy inflammatory response.

  • Gymnema Sylvestre Leaf Extract: A traditional Ayurvedic herb included as part of the hearing support blend.

  • GABA (Gamma-Aminobutyric Acid): A calming compound that supports relaxation.

The same blend also lists Guarana, African Mango, Eleutherococcus, Astragalus, Coleus Forskohlii, Grapefruit, and Panax Ginseng extracts, Raspberry Ketones, and amino acids such as L-Glutamine, L-Tyrosine, L-Arginine, Beta-Alanine, L-Ornithine, L-Tryptophan, and L-Carnitine, without the amount of each one. Chromium (as Chromium Picolinate) appears at 0.7 mcg, 2% of the Daily Value. The other ingredients are glycerin, water, organic lemon extract, stevia, xylitol, sodium benzoate, potassium sorbate, citric acid, and natural flavors.

Worth knowing: this is the same blend found in several metabolism drops, so many of its ingredients are better known for energy than for hearing.

Audifort Side Effects

Audifort is generally considered well tolerated and is intended for adults who want a gentle, natural approach to hearing and brain wellness, taken as a few drops a day.

The official website describes the formula as made with plant ingredients, easy to take, and non-habit forming, and the label states that it is manufactured in an FDA-registered facility. The liquid is sweetened with stevia and xylitol instead of sugar.

There are no widespread reports of serious side effects, but a few points deserve attention. The blend contains Guarana and Green Tea, two natural sources of caffeine, so people sensitive to caffeine may prefer to take it in the morning. Ginseng, GABA, and the amino acids may interact with some medications, so anyone who takes medication for blood pressure, blood sugar, mood, or sleep should show the label to their doctor. Sudden hearing loss, ear pain, or dizziness needs a doctor, not a supplement. It is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease, and individual results may vary.

According to the label, pregnant or nursing mothers, children under the age of 18, and individuals with a known medical condition should consult a physician before using Audifort.

Audifort Dosage

The recommended Audifort dosage is one or two droppers full a day. There are no capsules to swallow and nothing to prepare.

The label says to shake well before each use, take one or two droppers full daily, place the liquid in the mouth, and swallow. The official FAQ adds that a full dropper holds about 15 drops and that it can be placed under the tongue or dissolved in a glass of water or natural juice; one customer quoted on the website adds the drops to his morning coffee.

Each bottle holds 1 fl oz (30 ml), or 30 servings of 1 ml. The official website recommends consistent use for 4 months or longer, which is why it suggests the larger packages.

The label advises keeping the bottle away from heat, light, and humidity, storing it in a cool, dry place, and not using it if the safety seal is damaged or missing.

As with any dietary supplement, do not exceed the recommended dose. Audifort works best as part of a routine that includes protection from loud noise, good sleep, and regular hearing check-ups.
