BrainLive Ingredients

Every scoop of BrainLive (3 g) contains a 2.4 g proprietary blend of five ingredients listed on the Supplement Facts panel, chosen to support memory, focus, and mental clarity as part of a consistent daily routine:

  • Pea Starch: A plant starch that, according to the official website, feeds the beneficial gut bacteria and supports the gut-brain axis.

  • Baobab Fiber: A fiber from the fruit of Adansonia digitata, naturally rich in antioxidants, that helps protect cells against oxidative stress.

  • L-Arabinose: A natural plant sugar included to support steady energy levels throughout the day.

  • Konjac Glucomannan: A soluble fiber from the root of Amorphophallus konjac that slows digestion and supports a steady delivery of nutrients.

  • Spermidine: A compound naturally present in foods and in the body, associated with the natural process of cellular renewal.

Together, these ingredients work synergistically to support concentration, information retention, and a more active mind from within. Because it is a proprietary blend, the label shows the total amount but not the amount of each ingredient. The other ingredients are silicon dioxide, natural flavors, and stevia.
