Gelatide-1 Ingredients

Each capsule of Gelatide-1 lists three items on the Supplement Facts panel, and unlike many formulas, the two fibers show their exact amounts. These are the ingredients, chosen to support digestion, a healthy microbiome, and appetite control as part of a consistent daily routine:

  • Chicory Root Inulin (Cichorium intybus), 211 mg: A soluble prebiotic fiber that feeds beneficial gut bacteria and supports a feeling of fullness.

  • Potato Resistant Starch, 100 mg: A starch that is not digested in the small intestine and reaches the colon, where it works as food for the microbiome.

  • Bifidobacterium infantis: A well-known probiotic strain associated with digestive comfort.

  • Clostridium butyricum: A spore-forming probiotic that produces butyrate, a short-chain fatty acid that nourishes the gut lining.

  • Akkermansia muciniphila: A gut bacterium studied for its links to metabolic health and the gut barrier.

The three probiotic strains are grouped in a 36 mg probiotic blend, and the label does not give a CFU count or the amount of each strain. The other ingredients are hypromellose (the vegetarian capsule), microcrystalline cellulose, magnesium stearate, and silica. The label also says to refrigerate the bottle for optimal quality, which is common for products with live cultures.

One important note: the official sales presentation talks about a "pink gelatin" trick, but there is no gelatin on the label. This review follows the label, which is what comes in the bottle.
