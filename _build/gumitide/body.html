Gumitide Ingredients

Every gummy of Gumitide (2.5 g) contains a 525 mg proprietary blend of Apple Cider Vinegar and BHB salts, as listed on the Supplement Facts panel. The label gives the total of the blend, not the amount of each ingredient, and describes the official website's three key components:

  • Apple Cider Vinegar: A fermented apple product that the official website includes to help reduce blood sugar spikes after meals, which are linked to hunger and cravings.

  • BHB (beta-hydroxybutyrate): An exogenous ketone body that, according to the official website, signals the metabolism to use fat as an energy source, without requiring a strict ketogenic diet.

  • Calcium, Magnesium, and Sodium (as BHB salts): Mineral salts that carry the BHB and help replenish electrolytes; the label lists 1 mg of calcium, 0.75 mg of magnesium, and 2.5 mg of sodium per gummy.

Together, these ingredients are designed to support appetite control, steady energy, and fat metabolism from within.
