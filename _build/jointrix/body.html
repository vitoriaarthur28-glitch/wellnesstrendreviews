Jointrix Ingredients

Every capsule of Jointrix contains eight active ingredients listed on the Supplement Facts panel, led by 500 mg of Glucosamine Sulfate and chosen to support cartilage, flexibility, and joint comfort as part of a consistent daily routine:

  • Glucosamine Sulfate (500 mg): A compound naturally present in the body that supports the building blocks of healthy cartilage and joint lubrication.

  • Indian Frankincense (67 mg): A Boswellia serrata herb used for centuries to support a healthy inflammatory response and everyday mobility.

  • Chondroitin Sulfate (50 mg): A structural component of cartilage that works alongside glucosamine to help keep cartilage hydrated and elastic.

  • Turmeric (50 mg): A Curcuma longa root associated with inflammatory balance and overall joint wellness.

  • Quercetin (8 mg): A Sophora japonica flavonoid with antioxidant properties that helps protect cells against oxidative stress.

  • Methionine (8 mg): An essential amino acid that contributes to the normal maintenance of tissues.

  • Methylsulfonylmethane (MSM, 8 mg): A sulfur compound included to complement the formula's support for joint comfort.

  • Bromelain (8 mg): A pineapple stem enzyme included to support post-activity comfort.

Together, these ingredients work synergistically to support comfort, mobility, and a balanced inflammatory response from within. The other ingredients are hypromellose (vegetable capsule) and rice flour, and the label states that the formula contains shellfish (crayfish), the source of the glucosamine.

Jointrix is designed for daily use, offering a natural, non-prescription approach to supporting joint comfort, without stimulants or complicated routines.
