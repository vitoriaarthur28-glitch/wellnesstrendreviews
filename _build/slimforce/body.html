Slim Force Ingredients

Every serving of Slim Force (two capsules) contains an 800 mg proprietary blend of three beta-hydroxybutyrate (BHB) salts, as listed on the Supplement Facts panel. BHB is a ketone body, and these exogenous ketones are paired with minerals the body uses every day:

  • Magnesium Beta Hydroxybutyrate: BHB bound to magnesium, a mineral involved in energy production and muscle function.

  • Calcium Beta Hydroxybutyrate: BHB bound to calcium, included to deliver ketones together with a mineral that supports bone and muscle health.

  • Sodium Beta Hydroxybutyrate: BHB bound to sodium, an electrolyte that helps the body maintain fluid balance, which matters during low-carb eating.

Together, these ketone salts are designed to support energy production and mental clarity, especially during a low-carb or ketogenic lifestyle, according to the official website. The label gives the total amount of the blend, not the amount of each salt, and lists gelatin (capsule), rice flour, and magnesium stearate as the other ingredients.

Slim Force is designed for daily use, offering a natural, non-prescription approach to supporting weight management, as long as it is combined with a balanced diet and regular physical activity.
