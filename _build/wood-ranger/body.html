What Is Wood Ranger Leaf Blower And How Does It Work?

The Wood Ranger Power Leaf Blower is a compact, hand-held cordless blower sold online under the Wood Ranger tools brand. According to the official website, it is designed to clear leaves, dust, grass clippings, water and light debris from driveways, patios, garages and cars without a cord, gas tank or heavy backpack unit.

The tool looks like a cordless drill with a wide red nozzle in place of the chuck. A rechargeable lithium-ion battery pack clicks into the base of the handle, and the box and battery shown in the product photos are marked 24V. You charge the battery, slide it in, aim the nozzle and press the trigger to push a stream of air toward the area you want to clean.

The seller says the blower uses a "TurboJet" air system and a high-torque precision motor, and calls it "the most powerful cordless air blower ever designed". The website gives no airflow, air speed, motor wattage, battery capacity, runtime or charging time to back up that statement, so the power claims cannot be compared with other blowers.

Besides yard cleanup, the promotional article suggests drying a car after washing, blowing dust from vents, keyboards and blinds, clearing gutters, cleaning camping gear and inflating air mattresses or holiday decorations.

In practice, a small hand-held blower like this is best suited to light, quick jobs such as a patio, a short driveway, a garage floor or a car. Large lawns with deep, wet leaf piles usually call for a full-size blower, and how long one charge lasts will depend on the battery and how hard you use the trigger.

Wood Ranger Leaf Blower How To Use

According to the official website, using the blower takes three steps: charge, aim and blast. Fully charge the battery before the first use, slide it into the handle until it clicks, point the nozzle at the leaves, dust or water and press the trigger. Work from one side of the area to the other so the debris moves in one direction instead of scattering back.

Read the manual that comes with the tool before you start, and follow its charging instructions. Charge lithium-ion batteries on a hard, dry surface away from anything flammable, do not leave them charging unattended for long periods, and store them out of direct sun and freezing cold.

Wear safety glasses, since the air stream can throw dust, sand and small stones. Never point the nozzle at people, pets or your own face, keep children away while you work, and be careful near windows and parked cars. A dust mask is a good idea for dusty garages or workshops.

The promotional article says the blower can "spark your campfire instantly". A blower cannot light a fire; at most it pushes air into a fire that is already burning, which can send embers and sparks flying. Keep the plastic tool and its battery away from open flames, and follow local fire rules when camping.
