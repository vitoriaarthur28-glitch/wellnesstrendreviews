What Is Alpha Rise And How Does It Work?

Alpha Rise is sold as Male Enhancement Gummies, a chewable dietary supplement for men. The official product image shows a white bottle with a navy and orange label that reads “Stamina, Energy, Strength”, “225 MG Male Blend”, “Extra Strength” and “30 Gummies”.

The official website is a single order page. It presents the gummies in “Performance Pack” bundles and explains how orders, shipping and returns work, but it does not include a sales page, a Supplement Facts panel or a list of ingredients.

That means the content of the 225 mg “male blend” printed on the label is not disclosed online. The brand does not say which plants, vitamins or other compounds are inside, how much of each one is used, or how the formula is supposed to work.

The label words show how the brand positions the product: support for stamina, energy and strength in adult men, in an easy gummy format instead of capsules. These are marketing terms on the front of the bottle, each marked with an asterisk that refers to the standard FDA statement.

According to the terms on the official website, orders ship within 24 hours and arrive in 5 to 7 business days, and customer service answers by phone from Monday to Saturday. The order form accepts shipping addresses in the United States.

One detail matters before anyone orders: the same terms describe the purchase as a program that ships and bills again every 30 days until the customer cancels. This is explained in the refund policy section below.

As with any supplement, these statements have not been evaluated by the Food and Drug Administration, and Alpha Rise is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease. It is not a substitute for medical care, and men with ongoing concerns about energy or performance should talk to a doctor, since these can be signs of conditions that need proper treatment.

Is Alpha Rise A Scam?

Alpha Rise is a real product with an official order page, a phone line, a support email and written terms, so it is not an empty storefront. Still, the website leaves several gaps that every buyer should weigh.

First, there is no ingredient list. The label mentions a 225 mg male blend, but the website never says what it contains.

Second, the recurring billing: the terms describe a program that charges and ships again every 30 days until you cancel, while the order page shows only the package choices and the guarantee.

Third, the refund terms are stricter than the 30-day promise on the order page, with a phone call required, a processing fee and a restocking fee for each item. The footer of the order page also states that testimonials on the site use changed names and photos and that the individuals are paid.

Alpha Rise is a dietary supplement, not a treatment for any condition, and results vary. Men who take medication or have a health condition should speak with a doctor first.

If you still want to try it, order only from the official website, read the terms in full, note the cancellation number and check your statement every month.
