SlimTide Ingredients

Every capsule of SlimTide contains Chicory Root Inulin, Potato Resistant Starch, and a Probiotic Blend, a combination of prebiotics and probiotics chosen to support gut balance, satiety, and a healthy metabolism as part of a consistent daily routine:
